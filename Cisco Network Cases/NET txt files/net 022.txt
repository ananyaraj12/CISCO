Case ID: NET-022

Title: Sticky Client Roaming Failure Due to High Coverage Range

Symptom: Laptop0 retains connection to distant AccessPoint0 (-82 dBm, 1 bar) with severe latency instead of roaming to overhead AccessPoint1 (-42 dBm).

Topology: Laptop0 (Client) -> AccessPoint0 (Office-A) / AccessPoint1 (Conf-Room-B) -> Switch0 (2960-24TT)

Expected Fault: AccessPoint0 coverage range is set to maximum (140m), causing RF cell bleed into AccessPoint1 coverage area, trapping client association.

OSI Layer: Layer 1 / Layer 2

Concept: wireless-sticky-client-power-imbalance

Severity: Medium

Expected Next Command: check link metrics in PC Wireless / netsh wlan show interfaces

Expected Fix: Lower AccessPoint0 coverage range to 30m-50m on Port 1 to shrink RF footprint and allow client re-association.
